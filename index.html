About us

A small team fixing how safety gets taught

Parichay Tech builds interactive 3D training for warehouse and logistics teams. We started because safety inductions still lean on PDFs and toolbox talks that people forget by their second shift, and nobody could show who had actually understood them.

So we built the opposite: a warehouse your team walks through in the browser, where they spot hazards, respond to incidents, and get scored on what they did. Practical training, measurable results, no headset required.

Questions

Good to know before you book

Do my team need a VR headset or to install anything?

No. The warehouse runs in any modern browser, so your team just opens a link.

How are people scored?

Every hazard spotted and every incident response is scored. At the end, people see what was right, what wasn't, and why, so the feedback sticks.

What can trainers and managers see?

Completion across every starter and shift, the points where people struggle, and which hazards get missed most, so you can target your next toolbox talk and keep a clear record of who is ready for the floor.

Can it match our own site?

In our pilot, the forklift scenario was matched to the customer's real loading dock layout. Tell us about your sites in the booking form and we'll cover it in the demo.

What happens after I book a demo?

We email you with a few times that suit you, then give you a live walkthrough of the scenarios, scoring and trainer view.
